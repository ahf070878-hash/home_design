## Brand & Style

This design system delivers the structural rigor of architectural drafting merged with the institutional reliability of high-volume financial technology. Engineered for property seekers, licensed contractors, architects, and escrow-backed milestone financing in the Indonesian property sector, the interface instills absolute clarity, fiscal safety, and spatial intelligence.

The visual style blends **Modern Precision** with **Tactile Technical Detailing**:
- **Blueprint Drafting Precision:** Razor-sharp 1px keylines, subtle technical coordinates, and alignment grids referencing architectural CAD schematics.
- **FinTech Financial Reliability:** Uncompromising data hierarchy, tabular tracking of Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) flows, escrow state containers, and contract-ready status markers.
- **Curated Editorial Presence:** Generous whitespace framing high-resolution property photography, CAD elevation plans, and construction stage inspections.
- **Emotional Intent:** Transform the notoriously fragmented, risk-heavy process of land acquisition, architectural design, and home renovation into an engineered, milestone-verified digital journey.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate architectural cadence anchored by deep structural slates, sterile surfaces, financial emerald accents, and amber-gold site milestones:

- **Primary (`#059669` / Emerald-600):** Represents institutional trust, capital verification, escrow security, completion certificates, and authorized primary actions.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / Slate-900):** Anchors high-order typography, structural wireframes, CAD grid intersections, navigational chrome, and technical headers.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / Amber-500):** Used sparingly for construction hotspots, pending escrow releases, critical timeline milestones, and site warnings.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` / Slate-50):** The base background surface across views, evocative of unrolled drafting paper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces that create clean separation against the slate canvas.
- **Boundary & Grid Lines (`#E2E8F0` / Slate-200):** The definitive 1px perimeter token for cards, tabular rows, and technical floorplan overlays.
- **Muted Technical Copy (`#64748B` / Slate-500):** Used for unit metrics, blueprint scale tags, cadastral references, and secondary meta tags.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural precision of **JetBrains Mono**:

- **Editorial Headings & Structural Narrative:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tighter tracking at display scales (`-0.03em`) to mimic contemporary architectural publication layouts.
- **Financial & Dimension Tracking:** Indonesian Rupiah formatting (e.g., `Rp 1.450.000.000`), land square meters (`LT 120 m² / LB 180 m²`), milestone percentages, GPS coordinates, and CAD references enforce **JetBrains Mono** with tabular figure modes (`tnum`).
- **Technical Badges & KBLI/LPJK License Labels:** Displayed in uppercase `label-technical` with `letterSpacing: 0.06em` to maintain legibility at sub-12px sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional grid derived from standard architectural drafting matrices:

- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max-Width 1440px):** 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters with minimum 2rem outer canvas margins. Technical panels, split CAD-viewers, and escrow ledger dashboards use 4-column sidebars paired with 8-column workspaces.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px - 1024px):** 1rem gutters and 1.5rem margins. Cards adapt to 2-column distribution; escrow milestones fold into sequential vertical lists.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, <768px):** 1rem gutters and 1rem margins. Complex data layouts reflow into stacked, swipeable card carousels with fixed bottom transaction bars.
- **Micro-Cadence:** Component interiors strictly adhere to a 4px/8px rhythm. Structural metric readouts, form fields, and inspection checklist rows preserve high data density without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

To reflect physical architectural blueprints and enterprise financial tools, depth is established through sharp boundary lines and low-diffusion surface lifts rather than fuzzy, heavy shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`), completely flat with optional geometric dot-matrix or blueprint micro-grids (`16px x 16px` using `#E2E8F0` at 40% opacity).
- **Surface Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a continuous 1px solid border in Slate-200 (`#E2E8F0`). Subtle downward tension is delivered through `0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hotspots & Flying Modals):** Pure White with a 1px border in Slate-300 (`#CBD5E1`) and a directional lift: `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Escrow Drawer & Architectural Full-Screen Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an architectural backdrop overlay: Slate-900 at 65% opacity with an ambient blur of 4px.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, engineered corners (`roundedness: 1` / 4px base radius) to evoke the structural stability of steel framing, laser-measured materials, and drafting rulers:

- **Standard Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards, Metrics):** 4px (`0.25rem`) border radius, preserving sharp technical edges.
- **Containment Groups (Flooring Viewports, Contractor Dossiers):** `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`) for macro-level grouping cards.
- **Inspection Hotspots & Operational Avatars:** Strictly circular (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate interactive dimensional coordinate markers from structural content modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Escrow Fund, Sign SPK, Release Payment):** Emerald-600 background, crisp white typography, 4px radius, `font-weight: 600`. Hover state drops to Emerald-700; active state shifts to Emerald-800.
- **Secondary Structural (View Blueprints, Download BOQ):** Slate-900 background, white text, 4px radius.
- **Tertiary Outline (Compare Contractor):** 1px border Slate-300, background white, text Slate-700. Hover state introduces Slate-50 background and Slate-900 border.

### Metric Cards & Architectural Cards
- Pure White background, 1px Slate-200 border, 4px radius.
- Header contains `label-technical` with JetBrains Mono uppercase metadata (e.g., `LOT NO. 4B // RAB TAHAP II`).
- Content displays large JetBrains Mono financial totals or surface metrics accompanied by small Plus Jakarta Sans body annotations.

### Inspection & CAD Hotspots
- 24px circular interactive pin with Amber-500 fill, Slate-900 center ring, and an expanding 2px Amber-500 ping animation for active site issue flags.
- Selecting a hotspot opens an anchored Surface Level 2 card showing photographic proof, contractor verification signatures, and itemized material specs.

### Escrow Milestone Stepper
- Horizontal progression rail displaying discrete construction tranches (e.g., *Pondasi & Struktur*, *Dinding & Atap*, *Finishing Arsitektur*).
- Completed: Emerald-600 with checkmark.
- In Escrow/Active: Dual-ring Amber-500 with animated pulse.
- Locked/Future: Slate-200 outline with Slate-400 numerical index.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background White, 1px border Slate-300, 4px radius. Focus states apply a crisp Emerald-600 1px border alongside a 2px Emerald-100 external halo.
- Monospace input prefix for currency fields (`IDR` permanently visible in Slate-400).
- Dropdowns for structural classifications (e.g., IMB/PBG status, Sertifikat Hak Milik) feature tabular technical badges within the options menu.
